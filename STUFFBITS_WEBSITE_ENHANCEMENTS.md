## StuffBits Website – Enhancement Outline

### 1. Homepage Enhancements

#### 1.1 Hero / Above-the-fold
- **Goal**: Clearly explain what StuffBits does in 1–2 lines.
- **Content ideas**:
  - Short value statement (for example, “Embedded electronics and digital solutions from concept to product.”).
  - Primary CTA: “Talk to our experts” / “Get a quote”.
  - Secondary CTA: “View case studies” / “Explore services”.

#### 1.2 Client Impact / Case Studies Section
- **Position**: Below the stats section or services overview.
- **Section title examples**:
  - “Client Impact”
  - “Success Stories”
  - “How We Help Our Customers”
- **Content structure (cards)**:
  - Client or industry name.
  - 1–2 line “Challenge”.
  - 1–2 line “Solution”.
  - 1 bullet or line with measurable result if possible (for example, “30% faster time-to-market”).
  - Link: “Read case study” that goes to a detailed page in the portfolio/case studies section.

#### 1.3 Partners Section
- **Position**: Near the clients section, or above the footer.
- **Content**:
  - Short intro: “Partnering with the best to deliver reliable solutions.”
  - Row or grid of partner logos (chip vendors, cloud platforms, tool providers, and so on).
  - Optional “View all partners” link that points to a dedicated partners or about subsection.

#### 1.4 Resources / “What We Think” Section
- **Position**: After services or verticals, before the final CTA or footer.
- **Content**:
  - Section title: “Resources”, “Insights”, or “What We Think”.
  - 3–4 cards such as:
    - Featured blog posts.
    - Whitepaper, e‑book, or video if available.
  - Each card: title, short description, tag (Blog / Whitepaper / Video), and a CTA such as “Read more” or “Watch video”.

#### 1.5 Events / Webinars (Optional)
- **Position**: After Resources, or in a sidebar on the Blog or Resources page.
- **Content**:
  - Block with event title, date and time, 1–2 sentence description, and CTA (“Register now” or “Know more”).
  - Optional “View all events” link that points to an Events page.

#### 1.6 Newsletter / Stay in Touch CTA
- **Position**: Above the footer or inside the Resources section.
- **Content**:
  - Short text such as “Stay updated on embedded and IoT insights.”
  - Email input and button (for example, “Subscribe”).
  - Brief consent text for privacy.
  - Optional checkbox for “I agree to receive updates”.

### 2. Portfolio and Case Study Structure

#### 2.1 Portfolio Overview Page (`/portfolio`)
- **Hero**:
  - Title: “Our Projects” or “Work We’ve Delivered”.
  - Short description about project types (embedded hardware, firmware, IoT, and digital).
- **Content layout**:
  - Grid of project or case study cards.
  - Filters by industry or vertical (Automotive, Industrial, IoT, and so on).
- **Card fields**:
  - Project title.
  - Industry or vertical.
  - Short 1–2 line summary.
  - Link: “View case study”.

#### 2.2 Individual Case Study Page
- **Suggested sections**:
  - Title, industry, and client type.
  - Summary with 3–4 bullet key outcomes.
  - **Challenge**.
  - **Solution** (what StuffBits built).
  - **Results** (metrics if possible).
  - Technologies used (optional list).
  - Bottom CTA such as “Discuss a similar project”.

### 3. Careers Page Improvements

#### 3.1 Careers Hero
- **Content**:
  - Title: “Join Our Team”.
  - Short statement about culture and impact.
  - CTA: “View open positions”.

#### 3.2 Why Work at StuffBits
- **Section**:
  - 3–5 bullets or cards that cover:
    - Working on real embedded products.
    - Learning from senior engineers.
    - Culture, flexibility, and growth.
    - Tech stack highlights.

#### 3.3 Open Positions List
- **Layout**:
  - List or cards, each with:
    - Job title.
    - Location (On‑site, Remote, or Hybrid).
    - Experience level.
    - Short description (3–4 lines).
    - CTA such as “View details” or “Apply now” (email or form).
- **Optional**:
  - Filters or tags (Embedded, Firmware, Software, Internships).

#### 3.4 General Application CTA
- **Content**:
  - “Didn’t find a perfect role? Send us your resume at [email].”
